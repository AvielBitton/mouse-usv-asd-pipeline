  Restricted, repetitive patterns of behavior

 ≈4× more common in boys

slow, multi-step clinical pipeline.

  Standardized observation such as ADOS, across multiple sessions

Research questions
Three research questions — models, features,
and generalization.
RESEARCH QUESTIONS

Three questions frame the study

      MODELS
RQ1
      Which model family best classifies HET vs wild-type pups from their USVs — XGBoost, TabPFN,
      or sequence models?

      F E AT U R E S
RQ2
      Which acoustic characteristics drive the classification — and do two or three consistent
      features emerge?

      R E G I M E & G E N E R A L I Z AT I O N
RQ3
      How do the evaluation regime (subject-dependent vs -independent) and cohort (per-strain vs
      pooled) affect performance — above all, generalization to unseen mice?

      E N G I N E E R I N G G OA L
      Turn the inherited, non-runnable prototype into a reproducible, documented, end-to-end
      pipeline built on a single, auditable source of truth.
